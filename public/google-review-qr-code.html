How to make a Google review QR code

  1. Copy your review link. In your Google Business Profile, open Ask for reviews and copy the short review link. It opens the review box for your business.
  2. Paste the link. Drop it into the generator.
  3. Brand it. Add your colors, your logo and a Leave us a review label so it fits your counter or table.
  4. Print and place it. Download a high-resolution PNG or SVG and put it on table tents, receipts, cards or the shop window.

Why it collects more reviews

Most happy customers would leave a review if it were easy, but typing your business name into Google and finding the review box is friction. A QR code removes that: one scan opens the star rating for your business while the customer is still in the moment. Place it where satisfaction peaks, such as after a meal, at checkout or inside the package, and reviews add up.

Because the code is a plain static QR, it keeps working with no monthly fee and no expiry, and you can reprint it as often as you like at no cost.

Questions

What is a vCard QR code?

It is a QR code that holds contact details in the standard vCard format. When someone scans it, their phone offers to save your name, phone, email and other fields straight into contacts.

Does it add me to contacts automatically?

The phone opens a new contact card prefilled with your details and the person taps save. It never adds anything without them confirming.

Which fields can I include?

First and last name, company, job title, phone, email and website. Leave any blank field empty and it is simply skipped.

Does the code expire?

No. The details are baked into the image, so the code works forever with no account and no fee. If your details change, generate a new code.

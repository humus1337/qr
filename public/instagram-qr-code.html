How to make an Instagram QR code

  1. Copy your profile link. It is instagram.com followed by your username, for example instagram.com/yourname.
  2. Paste the link. Drop it into the generator.
  3. Brand it. Use your brand colors or a gradient and drop your logo in the center.
  4. Download and share. Save PNG or SVG and add it to flyers, packaging, a poster or a story.

Works from any camera, not just the app

Instagram has its own in-app nametag, but it is built for scanning inside Instagram. A standard QR code to your profile link opens from any phone camera, so someone who does not have the app open can still reach your page. That makes it a better fit for print: business cards, product packaging, event signage and shop windows.

Want to send people to a specific post, reel or a link-in-bio page instead? Paste that URL rather than your profile, and the code points exactly there.

Questions

How is this different from Instagram's own QR code?

Instagram's in-app nametag is designed to be scanned inside the Instagram app. This makes a standard QR code to your profile link, so it opens from any phone camera, and you can brand it with your own colors and logo.

Can I link to a specific post or reel?

Yes. Paste the full URL of the post, reel or highlight instead of your profile link, and the code opens exactly that.

Does the code expire?

No. The link is baked into the image, so the code works as long as your profile exists. There is no account and no fee.

Can I add my logo?

Yes. Drop a logo into the center and the code stays scannable thanks to high error correction, with no watermark on the download.

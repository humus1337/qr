  1. Enter the address. Add the email address the message should go to.
  2. Prefill subject and message. Optionally set a subject line and a message body so the draft is ready to send.

One scan to reach your inbox

Instead of printing an address people have to type by hand, an email QR code opens a ready-to-go draft. Set the subject to something like Feedback or Support so replies land sorted, and prefill a short message to guide the sender. It is a tidy fit for posters, packaging inserts, receipts and event signage.

Questions

What happens when someone scans it?

Their phone opens the mail app with a new message already addressed to you, with any subject and body you set prefilled. They review it and send.

Can I preset the subject and message?

Yes. Fill in the subject and message fields and both appear in the draft, which is handy for feedback, support requests and RSVPs.

Does it send the email automatically?

No. It only opens a draft. The person always reviews and sends the message themselves, so nothing is sent without them.

Is it free with no watermark?

Yes. The code is free, has no watermark, and never expires. It is generated entirely in your browser.
